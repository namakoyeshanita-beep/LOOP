Our shows

Four shows, each on its own day, so you always know what is coming.

Every Monday

The Campuz Loop Podcast

Honest talks with students, graduates and young founders. The theme changes every week:

  • Money and business
  • Life after campus
  • Health and fitness
  • Tech and innovation
Watch on YouTube
Tuesday and Thursday

Campus Street Quiz

We walk around campus with quick questions. Answer right and win a prize.

Watch on TikTok
Every Friday

Campus Report

Five minutes of the week's campus news, deadlines from the Study Hub and new opportunities.

Every two weeks

Campus Tour

We show the places, food spots and people of Victoria University. Great for freshers.

Watch on YouTube
