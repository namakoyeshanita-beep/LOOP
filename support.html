How it works

  1. A student applies using the form below.
  2. The Dean of Students' office checks the fees balance.
  3. The student agrees to what we post: first name, programme and story only.
  4. We share the campaign on this website and on WhatsApp.
  5. Money is paid straight to the university with the student's registration number.
  6. We post an update when the target is reached.

How to give

Mobile Money

Send any amount to our fees support code 255600. Use the student's first name as the reason.

Pay the university

Pay at the bank with the student's registration number. We share it privately after checks.

Share

No money right now? Share the campaign. One share can reach a hundred students.
