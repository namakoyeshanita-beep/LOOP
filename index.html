About us

Who we are

Campuz Loop is a student platform started by Victoria University students, for Victoria University students.

We began by sharing campus news, a podcast and street games online. This website adds tools that help students every day.

Mission

To keep every Victoria University student informed, connected and supported.

Vision

A campus where no student misses a deadline, an opportunity or their studies because of fees.

Values

Honest, helpful, fun and respectful of every student's privacy.
